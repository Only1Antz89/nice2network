# n2 online-safety assessment register

This register is an operational starting point, not a claim of legal sign-off. The accountable n2 safety owner must complete, approve and periodically review each record in `compliance_assessments` before admitting 16–17-year-old members.

## Required records

1. **Children's access assessment** — document whether children are likely to access the service, the evidence used, the age-assurance approach, and the review date.
2. **Children's risk assessment** — assess discovery, adult contact, messaging, invitations, meetings, location disclosure, harmful content, reporting, recommender systems and foreseeable misuse. Record likelihood, impact, controls, residual risk and owner.
3. **Age-appropriate privacy review** — verify high-privacy defaults, data minimisation, geolocation controls, profiling exclusions, understandable notices and parental considerations.

## Controls implemented in the product

- Date of birth is self-declared and converted to an age band; precise birth dates are excluded from analytics.
- 16–17 profiles default to restricted visibility, hidden location and behavioural matching disabled.
- Unsolicited adult messages and project invitations to teens are blocked and create safety-risk records.
- Adult/teen contact requires teen initiation and a shared project.
- Mixed-age Meets require a project-linked group of at least three; location is withheld from the stored meeting record.
- Moderators access reported content through evidence snapshots, with privileged reads and decisions appended to an immutable audit log.

## Approval checklist

- Assign an accountable safety owner and independent appeal reviewer.
- Confirm policy categories, response targets and emergency escalation contacts.
- Test the teen flows with representative users and document outcomes.
- Record the lawful bases, retention schedule and processor inventory.
- Obtain qualified UK online-safety and data-protection review before launch.
- Schedule review after material feature changes and at least annually.

Reference guidance: Ofcom protection-of-children duties and the ICO Children's Code linked in the implementation plan.
